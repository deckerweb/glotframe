## Getting started and everyday use

### Do I need GlotPress?

Yes. The translation workspace requires active GlotPress 4.x and readable permalinks. GlotFrame can still be activated without GlotPress. Prepare and save settings and layouts while dependent features pause.

### How do I create headers and footers?

Open GlotFrame → Settings → Header, footer and content. Create block layouts, publish them and assign them to the desired regions.

### Does it work with my theme or page builder?

GlotFrame provides its own frame using WordPress blocks. Content created with Bricks, Elementor or other page builders is not supported as GlotFrame layouts.

### Does GlotFrame change my translations?

No. Projects, translations, permissions, forms, downloads and API responses remain managed by GlotPress.

### What do the statistics mean?

They show current translation tasks and retained contribution records. Project completion is weighted. They are not a complete activity history or quality ranking.

### Does GlotFrame support Multisite?

Yes. Each website manages its own settings and layouts, including with network activation.

### What happens when I deactivate or delete it?

Deactivation preserves all data. Deletion preserves settings and layouts by default. Optional deletion affects GlotFrame content only; GlotPress data is retained.

## More answers by topic

### Where can I find patterns and blocks?

Find seven patterns under GlotFrame layouts and five dynamic blocks under GlotFrame in the block inserter. [Details](Layouts-and-design.md).

### Why is my layout missing from the selector?

Only published layouts without passwords are eligible. Draft, private and trashed layouts remain hidden. [Details](Layouts-and-design.md).

### Can I reuse or duplicate a layout?

Yes. One layout can serve multiple regions. Duplicate as draft creates an independent, initially unassigned copy. [Details](Layouts-and-design.md).

### How do I change colors and the background?

Choose a preset and adjust its values. The outer background is visible in boxed mode; images come from the Media Library. [Details](Layouts-and-design.md).

### Why do old links stop working after changing the path?

GlotFrame does not create a redirect from an old custom path. Update menus and bookmarks. [Details](Portal-and-toolbar.md).

### How do I choose the toolbar position?

Choose its position in Admin & Toolbar and select a present reference for before/after. A missing reference uses the standard position. [Details](Portal-and-toolbar.md).

### How do I choose German informal or formal?

Choose the GlotPress interface language. It affects neither WordPress administration nor your project translations. [Details](Projects-and-translation.md).

### Are personal contributions an activity log?

No. Counts cover retained records attributed to you. Imports and deletions limit what they can represent. [Details](Statistics.md).

### Why are proposals not another completion category?

Proposals and warnings can overlap other figures. They are not additional portions of overall completion. [Details](Statistics.md).

### Which data is sent to external services?

The updater contacts GitHub. The additional Library online catalog is optional and initially off. GlotFrame adds no telemetry. [Details](Administration-and-data.md).

### Why are commands missing from the palette?

Commands appear only for authorized website administrators where WordPress provides its native palette; older versions may limit this to the block editor. [Details](Administration-and-data.md).

### How do I report a bug or security issue?

Use Issues for bugs and Discussions for questions. Submit security details only through GitHub’s private reporting. [Details](Help-and-security.md).
